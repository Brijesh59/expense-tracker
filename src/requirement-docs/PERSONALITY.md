🌕 Luma Personality System
🧭 Core Idea

Luma is a calm, observant companion who helps you understand your money — without judging you.

Not:

a strict accountant ❌
a motivational guru ❌
a data dashboard ❌
🧠 1. Archetype
Primary: The Guide
Helps you see clearly
Doesn’t force decisions
Gives perspective, not आदेश
Secondary: The Friend (low-key)
Relatable
Slightly witty
Never overbearing
🎯 2. Tone Pillars (Non-negotiable)
1. Calm
No urgency unless critical
No panic words

❌ “Overspending alert!!!”
✅ “This is running a bit high”

2. Non-judgemental
Never blame the user

❌ “You exceeded your budget”
✅ “This went a bit over”

3. Observational (not preachy)
State patterns, don’t give lectures

❌ “You should stop eating out”
✅ “Dining is higher than usual this week”

4. Concise
1–2 lines max
No paragraphs
5. Slightly Human
Light warmth, tiny personality

✅ “That adds up 👀”
✅ “Happens 🙂”

🗣️ 3. Voice Rules (Very Practical)
Always:
Use “this” instead of “you” where possible
Use softeners: “a bit”, “slightly”, “looks like”
Prefer present tense
Avoid:
Commands (“reduce”, “stop”, “limit”)
Financial jargon (“liabilities”, “expenditure”)
Harsh words (“failed”, “bad”, “exceeded”)
🎭 4. Emotional Range (When to say what)
🟢 When user is doing well

Tone: appreciative, subtle

“Nice, this is steady”
“You’re keeping things balanced”
“Looks good so far”

👉 Never overhype

🟡 When things are drifting

Tone: observant, slightly alert

“This is picking up a bit”
“Spending is trending higher here”
“Might be worth keeping an eye”
🔴 When user is overspending

Tone: calm, supportive

“That went a bit over — happens”
“This crossed your budget”
“A little high here”

👉 No guilt

🧘 When user is struggling (VERY IMPORTANT)

Tone: empathetic, grounding

“Rough week? It’s okay”
“No stress — tomorrow’s a reset”
“Tracking itself is progress”

👉 This is retention

🧬 5. Personality Traits (Consistent Behavior)
👀 Observant

Luma notices patterns:

“You tend to spend more on weekends”
“Evenings are your high-spend time”
🧘 Calm under pressure

Even in worst case:

Never escalates emotionally
Always feels stable
🤝 Supportive, not controlling
Suggests, never instructs
🪶 Lightweight
Doesn’t flood user with info
One insight at a time
⚡ 6. Microcopy Style Guide
Buttons
“Add expense” → “Log expense”
“Create budget” → “Set budget”
Labels
“Remaining” → “Left”
“Exceeded” → “Over”
Empty states
“Start tracking your expenses” ❌
“Let’s add your first expense” ✅
🧠 7. What Luma NEVER Does
❌ Never shames
No guilt language
No “you should”
❌ Never overwhelms
No multiple alerts at once
No data dumps
❌ Never pretends to be too smart
No fake AI tone
No “Based on advanced analysis…”
🧩 8. Example: Same Situation, Different Personalities
❌ Typical App

“You have exceeded your dining budget by 23%.”

❌ Overfriendly App

“Oopsie!! You’re spending too much on food 😂😂”

✅ Luma

“Dining has gone a bit over this month”

👉 That’s your edge.

🏆 9. One-Line Personality Test

Before shipping any copy, ask:

“Would a calm, smart friend say this out loud?”

If not → rewrite.

💡 Final Insight (Most Important)

You’re not building:

a finance app

You’re building:

a feeling of control

If you stay consistent with this personality across:

nudges
insights
empty states
onboarding

Luma will feel cohesive, which is exactly what most clones lack.
