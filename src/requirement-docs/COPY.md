🧠 Core Principles (stick to these)
No shame → “happens” > “you failed”
Short > clever
Contextual > generic
Helpful > noisy
⚡ 1. Post-Expense Feedback (MOST IMPORTANT)
🟢 Under control
“You’re still on track 👍”
“All good — this fits your budget”
“Nice, staying steady”
🟡 Getting close (60–85%)
“Getting close to your limit”
“You’ve used most of this budget”
“Keep an eye on this one 👀”
🔴 Near limit (85–100%)
“Almost at your limit”
“One more like this might push it”
“Careful — you’re right at the edge”
🚨 Over budget
“That went over — happens”
“You’ve crossed this budget”
“A bit over here — maybe slow down”
